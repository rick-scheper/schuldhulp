# Box 3 werkelijk rendement: novelle 37 053 (as of 9 Nov 2026)

Findings for [How detailed is the tax model?](https://github.com/rick-scheper/schuldhulp/issues/7). They complement §5.3 of `dutch-student-loans.md`.

## What the novelle changes

Sources: [MvT 37053-3](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55228), [wetsvoorstel 37053-2](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55225), [nota n.a.v. verslag](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D56385), [aanbiedingsbrief 3-11-2026](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55254), [RvS advies](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D55229).

- **Financial instruments** (shares, bonds, funds, options, derivatives) are taxed on **realised** gains instead of annual unrealised gains.
  - Existing holdings get a step-up: their acquisition price is set to market value on 1-1-2029.
  - Partial sales use the average acquisition price.
- **Still taxed annually** on the year's result: bank balances, receivables, debts, crypto, insurance and other assets. The cabinet expects to move these to realisation taxation from 2030 in a separate bill.
- **Start date:** 1-1-2029. The forfait with tegenbewijs runs through 2028. The nota leaves a 2028 start open, but 2029 is the stated plan.
- **Unchanged:** the 36% rate, the €1,800 heffingsvrij resultaat, loss carry-forward, the €500 loss threshold, and paid debt interest counting as negative income.
- **Dropped from the draft:**
  - cutting the heffingsvrij vermogen to €30,846;
  - a forfait of 7.87% for other assets;
  - a heffingsvrij resultaat of €1,000.
- **Funding moves to box 2** from 2027 (excessief lenen, box 2 rates).
- **TK vote** planned for 12-11-2026.

## Study-loan interest

- Neither 36 748 nor 37 053 mentions studieschulden.
- Under the general rules, DUO interest is negative box 3 income:
  - art. 5.5 defines debts and does not exclude study loans;
  - art. 5.7(2) makes interest paid on debts negative income;
  - there is no debt threshold.
- This is an inference, not an explicit statement.
- **Risk:** debts are valued at economic value (MvT §3.10). Kwijtschelding could then count as a positive change in value. The remission exemption in art. 5.14 covers only claims the debtor cannot fully pay (financieel onvolwaardige vorderingen). **Unresolved.**

## Indexation and 2027 parameters

- **Heffingsvrij resultaat and loss threshold:** indexed from 2029 under art. 10.1 and art. VI of 36 748. Not verified: whether art. VI is adjusted for the new 2029 start.
- **Belastingplan 2027 (37 022):** contains no box 3 parameter changes.
- **2027 forfait percentages and heffingsvrij vermogen:** not published in primary sources. The 2026 bank and debt percentages are still provisional.
