# Domain glossary

- **Studieschuld** — outstanding DUO student debt (principal + accrued interest).
- **Regime** — the set of DUO repayment rules that applies to a borrower. v1 supports only **SF35**: first hbo/wo student finance in/after 2015-2016 (leenstelsel), or first student finance of any kind in/after 2023-2024 (basisbeurs); 35-year term.
- **Aanloopfase** — the 2-year grace period after studies before repayment starts; interest accrues.
- **Terugbetaalfase** — the repayment period (35 years under SF35).
- **Wettelijk maandbedrag** — the statutory monthly payment DUO computes from the debt, rate, and remaining term (recalculated in repayment years 1 and 4, then every 5 years).
- **Termijn** — the monthly amount actually due to DUO: the wettelijk maandbedrag, unless draagkracht caps it lower.
- **Rentevaste periode** — a block of 5 calendar years in which the DUO rate stays fixed. The first one starts with the aanloopfase (1 January after student finance stops), so the periodes line up with the recalculations in repayment years 4, 9, …; each new periode takes the SF35 rate of its first year (Wsf art. 6.4 lid 3; DUO). While still studying the rate resets every calendar year. The user may enter the current periode's end year (Mijn DUO); otherwise it is derived.
- **DUO-rente** (SF35) — the rate for calendar year Y: the October Y−2 to September Y−1 average of the 5-year Dutch government yield, rounded to 2 decimals, never below 0% (Wsf art. 6.3). The yield itself may be negative. The **huidige DUO-rente** is pre-filled with the published rate for the start year of the user's current periode.
- **Rente blijft gelijk** — the deterministic mode (under Geavanceerd) in which every later rentevaste periode keeps the huidige DUO-rente; used for validation against DUO's rekenhulp and as an explainer.
- **Rentepad** — the sequence of DUO rates a borrower faces over the remaining term in one scenario, derived from that scenario's 5-year government bond yield. Until the Monte Carlo exists the app uses one expected yield path (the Vasicek path without spreiding).
- **Draagkracht** — income-based cap on the monthly payment (SF35: 4% of the toetsingsinkomen above the draagkrachtvrije voet, per year, divided over 12 months; Wsf art. 6.10). After a year in which draagkracht capped the termijn, DUO recalculates the wettelijk maandbedrag on 1 January over the months left (art. 6.15). With no income entered there is no cap.
- **Draagkrachtvrije voet** — the income threshold above which draagkracht starts: for SF35 100% of the minimum wage for a single debtor without children (€28,405.73 in 2027). v1 models only the single case and indexes the voet with wage growth (2.4%).
- **Peiljaar** — the year whose income DUO uses for draagkracht: two calendar years before the year of the termijn (t−2, Wsf art. 6.12).
- **Inkomensgroei** — the yearly nominal growth of the user's income path (default 2.4%, CP wage inflation); set under Geavanceerd.
- **Aflosvrije periode** — up to 60 months the borrower may skip repayment; interest keeps accruing and the term is extended by the months used.
- **Kwijtschelding** — forgiveness of the remaining debt at the end of the term.
- **Extra aflossen** — voluntary repayment above the monthly amount (the "pay off" strategy).
- **Surplus** — the monthly amount the user can spare on top of the required monthly payment; the money a strategy allocates.
- **Inleg** — an optional one-off amount (existing savings) allocated at the start, alongside the surplus.
- **Vrijgekomen geld** — the part of the termijn a strategy no longer pays compared with the 0%-aflossen debt path, because extra aflossen lowered the termijn or cleared the debt. It is allocated like the surplus (so every strategy spends the same cash each month); once the debt is cleared it goes into the strategy's investment split, for 100% extra aflossen into equities unless overridden.
- **Toetsingsinkomen** — the income DUO bases draagkracht on (roughly the verzamelinkomen).
- **Vastrentend** — a stable investment that earns one fixed annual rate (e.g. a deposito or staatsobligatie held to maturity). Its **soort vastrentend product** (Geavanceerd) sets the box 3 category: a deposito (default) is a banktegoed, a staatsobligatie an overige bezitting.
- **Dividendlek** — dividend withholding tax lost inside an index fund, which the investor cannot reclaim; a hidden yearly drag on the equity return on top of the fund's TER.
- **Strategy** — a plan for the surplus and inleg: extra aflossen, invest in equities, vastrentend, or a mix.
- **Mix** — a user-defined strategy: a share to extra aflossen, the rest split between equities and vastrentend.
- **Scenario** — one simulated future (interest-rate path, market returns, income path) a strategy is evaluated against. Called a **toekomst** in the UI.
- **Forfait** — the current box 3 regime: tax on a fictive return per asset category (banktegoeden, overige bezittingen, schulden) on the holdings of 1 January, capped at the actual return via **tegenbewijs**. The model holds the 2026 rate and percentages for every forfait year.
- **Tegenbewijs** — when the werkelijk rendement of a year (income and value changes, also unrealised, minus interest on debts; at least 0; no heffingsvrij vermogen) is lower than the forfait voordeel, that werkelijk rendement is taxed instead. So the yearly tax is min(forfait tax, 36% × actual return).
- **Heffingsvrij vermogen** — the box 3 wealth (after netting deductible debts) that the forfait leaves untaxed: €59,357 per person in 2026, indexed in the model with its inflation assumption.
- **Schuldendrempel** — the part of box 3 debts that is not deductible: €3,800 per person in 2026, indexed like the heffingsvrij vermogen. The full DUO balance counts as a box 3 debt.
- **Werkelijk rendement** — the planned box 3 regime (target 2029, not yet law): tax on actual annual return; gains on financial instruments (shares, funds, bonds) are taxed only when realised, while bank balances and debts are taxed on their annual result.
- **Overig box 3-vermogen** — the user's box 3 wealth outside the strategy (e.g. existing savings not used as inleg), default €0; treated as banktegoeden and held constant in real terms. It decides how much of the heffingsvrij vermogen the strategy can use. Box 3 tax is taken from the strategy's pot at each year end, or from the surplus while the pot is empty.
- **Eindvermogen** — the after-tax value of a strategy's pot at the end of the terugbetaalfase, with the pot treated as sold; by then the debt is either paid off or forgiven, so nothing is subtracted. The number strategies are scored on.
- **Kwijtgescholden bedrag** — the debt left at the end of the term that is forgiven; shown for information, never added to eindvermogen.
- **Euro's van nu** — amounts deflated to today's purchasing power with the model's price inflation assumption; the default unit for results.
- **Baseline** — the 100% extra aflossen strategy, against which every other strategy is compared.
- **Winkans** — the share of scenarios in which a strategy ends with a higher eindvermogen than the baseline in the same scenario.
- **Ongeveer gelijk** — the verdict when two strategies' median eindvermogen differs by too little to call a winner.
- **Doorsnee uitkomst** — the user-facing name for a strategy's median eindvermogen: half of the scenarios end better, half worse. Not an expected value (mean); never phrased as "naar verwachting".
- **Break-even** — the constant yearly index return at which 100% equities ends level with 100% extra aflossen.
- **Hypotheekmoment** — the moment, in years from now, at which the user expects to apply for a mortgage; the mortgage side note is evaluated there.
- **Koopbudget** — what a strategy leaves the user to buy a house with at the hypotheekmoment: the maximum mortgage given the remaining DUO termijn, plus the after-tax pot. The pot is either kept as eigen inbreng or used to pay off DUO first, whichever gives the larger budget. Shown for information, never scored.
- **Gedeelde berekening** — a calculation opened from a share link and not yet adopted as the user's own inputs; shown with a banner, and the user's saved inputs stay untouched until they choose "Overnemen".
