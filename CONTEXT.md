# Domain glossary

- **Studieschuld** — outstanding DUO student debt (principal + accrued interest).
- **Regime** — the set of DUO repayment rules that applies to a borrower. v1 supports only **SF35** (leenstelsel, started studying 2015–2023: 35-year term).
- **Aanloopfase** — the 2-year grace period after studies before repayment starts; interest accrues.
- **Terugbetaalfase** — the repayment period (35 years under SF35).
- **Wettelijk maandbedrag** — the statutory monthly payment DUO computes from the debt, rate, and remaining term.
- **Draagkracht** — income-based reduction of the monthly payment.
- **Aflosvrije periode** — months the borrower may skip repayment (term is not extended).
- **Kwijtschelding** — forgiveness of the remaining debt at the end of the term.
- **Extra aflossen** — voluntary repayment above the monthly amount (the "pay off" strategy).
- **Strategy** — a plan for a monthly surplus: extra aflossen, invest in equities, invest in bonds, or a mix.
- **Scenario** — one simulated future (interest-rate path, market returns, income path) a strategy is evaluated against.
